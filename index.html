Fair play

Cosmetic only. Always.

ChessFX changes how Chess.com looks and sounds. It never helps you play.

  • No engine, evaluation, hints or move suggestions on Chess.com.
  • Never makes or changes a move. Effects only react to moves already played.
  • During your game it doesn't read the position; reviews and the Lichess hand-off appear only after it ends.
  • Use it at your own risk under Chess.com's terms: they restrict third-party software.

Privacy

Your data stays with you.

No tracking and no ads. As a guest, nothing leaves your browser.

  • Settings, looks and game history are saved in your browser.
  • An account is optional and only syncs your ChessFX settings between browsers.
  • Game history syncs only if you turn it on. Delete your account any time.

Questions

Is ChessFX allowed on Chess.com?

ChessFX is purely cosmetic and never assists your play. Chess.com's terms restrict third-party software in general, so use it at your own risk. It is not affiliated with Chess.com.

Does it slow my board down?

Animated boards run on your graphics card and lower their quality automatically if your computer struggles, in bullet games, or when you ask your system to reduce motion.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers on desktop. Chess.com's mobile app isn't supported.

When can I install it?

ChessFX is launching soon on the Chrome Web Store. It will be free, and this page will link to it the day it's live.

Does it cost anything?

No. Every board, piece set, effect and sound pack is free, and you don't need an account.

Why does it ask for access to chess.com?

To restyle chess.com pages and swap Chess.com's own board, piece and sound files for the ones you pick. It doesn't run on other websites.

How do I remove it?

Right-click the ChessFX icon and choose Remove. Chess.com goes back to exactly how it was.
