Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

The short version

ChessFX is a cosmetic extension. As a guest, everything it stores stays in your browser and nothing is sent to us. If you create an account, we store your email address and the settings you choose to sync, only to sync them between your browsers.

What ChessFX stores in your browser

  • Your settings, saved looks, favourite boards and pieces, and rating goals.
  • Images and sounds you upload.
  • Your game history: finished chess.com games, including your opponent's username, the moves (PGN), ratings, the result and any notes you add.

What an account sends to our server

  • Your email address and a password (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase; we never see or store your password).
  • Your settings, saved looks, favourites and goals.
  • Your game history, only if you turn on "Sync my game history" in Settings → Account. It includes your opponents' usernames and the moves of your games. Turning it off removes your synced games from the server.

Uploaded images and sounds are not synced.

How we use it

Only to sign you in and sync your ChessFX data between your browsers. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, share it with third parties (other than Supabase, which stores it for us), or use it for anything unrelated to syncing.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services ChessFX talks to

  • Chess.com: ChessFX changes how chess.com pages look in your browser. It does not send anything to chess.com.
  • Lichess: only when you click "Analyze on Lichess" after a game, ChessFX sends that game's moves to Lichess's public import.

Your choices

  • Use ChessFX as a guest: no account, nothing leaves your browser.
  • Export your data: Settings → Account → Export my data.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete account. This deletes your account and synced data from our server immediately.
  • Sign out and clear this device: Settings → Account → Sign out.

Children

You must be 13 or older to create an account (16 where your country's law requires it). Younger players can use ChessFX as a guest, which collects nothing.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. On our server each account can read and write only its own data. Your sign-in token is kept in the extension's own storage and is never shared with web pages.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
